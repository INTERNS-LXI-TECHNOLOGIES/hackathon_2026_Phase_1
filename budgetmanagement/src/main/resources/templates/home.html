About Budget Tracker

A Budget Tracker helps individuals monitor and manage their financial activities effectively. By recording income and expenses, users can analyze their spending habits and maintain better financial discipline.

This application allows you to add transactions, organize them by categories, view sorted financial data, and analyze your budget through interactive dashboards. Maintaining a budget helps you plan savings, avoid unnecessary expenses, and achieve long-term financial stability.
